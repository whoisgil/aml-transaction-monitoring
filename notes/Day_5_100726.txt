Case and analyst workflow implemented.

Added cases, case_alerts, and case_notes with workflow statuses, timestamps,
analyst notes, and final dispositions. Alerts for the same matched account
share one active case, while later activity can open a new case after the
earlier case is closed.

Alert assignment is transactional: the case, case-to-alert link, and CASED
alert status are committed together. A failed attempt rolls back without
leaving a partial case or link. Duplicate alert links are prevented by the
database.

Analyst notes require a named analyst and cannot be added after closure.
Closing requires recordDisposition, which records SAR_FILED or NO_SAR and
prevents the original analyst decision from being overwritten. The final SAR
decision remains human-controlled.

Added unit and MySQL integration coverage for grouping, status changes, notes,
case closure, duplicate links, and disposition protection. Full suite: 24
tests passing.
