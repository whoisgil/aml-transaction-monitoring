Investigation interface implemented.

Chose Streamlit and added a case-first analyst review interface in sar_app.py.
The queue shows active cases by default, can include closed cases, and summarizes
each case's matched account, status, alert count, and peak observed rule value.

The case detail view shows grouped alerts, decoded rule evidence, involved
sending and receiving accounts, evidence transactions, and analyst-note history.
Closed cases remain available for review without exposing controls that could
change the final decision.

Added analyst actions for moving an OPEN case to IN_REVIEW, saving named notes,
and recording a confirmed SAR_FILED or NO_SAR disposition. Status transitions
record the analyst as an audit note, and the status update and audit note commit
together or roll back together.

Added investigation query helpers for the case queue, case records, alerts,
transactions, and notes. JSON transaction evidence is decoded before reaching
the interface, and overlapping evidence transaction IDs are deduplicated.

Updated run_detection.py to group open alerts after persistence. Grouping also
runs when alerts already exist so an interrupted workflow can be resumed.

Added requirements.txt with pinned PyMySQL, pytest, and Streamlit versions.
Expanded unit and MySQL integration coverage for queue counts, case lookup,
evidence decoding, evidence-transaction ordering and deduplication, closed-case
filtering, analyst history, status attribution, and safe connection cleanup.
The automated suite now contains 34 tests.

Day 6 objective complete. Next up: Day 7 — additional detection rules and
PaySim-label evaluation.
